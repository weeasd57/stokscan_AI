# How EGX Bots Helps Customers

Here is a ready-to-use answer for the Acquire.com listing question: **"How does your startup help customers?"**

---

### Core Value Proposition (For Acquire Listing)

**EGX Bots** helps retail stock market investors in the Egyptian Stock Exchange (EGX) make smarter, emotion-free, and profitable trading decisions through automation, machine learning, and instant messaging alerts.

Here is how the platform solves critical problems for its customers:

#### 1. Saves Time via Automated Market Scanning
Analyzing 200+ listed stocks daily on the EGX is exhausting for retail traders. EGX Bots runs a fully automated daily pipeline that calculates 50+ technical indicators, sentiment-scores financial news, and scans the entire market in seconds, delivering the most promising setups directly to the user.

#### 2. Delivers High-Probability AI Signals
Instead of trading based on hearsay or social media rumors, customers receive quantitative trade recommendations generated by custom Machine Learning models (LightGBM/Optuna) trained on years of historical EGX data. The platform only suggests trades meeting strict precision and volume thresholds.

#### 3. Enhances Risk Management and Discipline
Retail traders often fail because they don’t set or respect stop-losses and profit targets. EGX Bots solves this by constantly tracking their active portfolio positions. The moment a stock price hits their target profit (TP) or stop-loss (SL) level on the daily bar, the system triggers an immediate alert via Telegram, reminding the user to exit and lock in gains or cut losses.

#### 4. Validates Setups with Historical Success Rates ("Shabah Dah")
Before entering a trade, customers can see how similar chart patterns performed in the past. The platform search engine (Cosine Similarity/KNN) finds the top 10 matching historical setups over the last 10 years and displays their win rates, giving traders data-backed confidence.

#### 5. Protects Capital with EGX-Specific Safeguards
EGX Bots has built-in circuit-breaker halting detectors, low-volume dry-up filters, and market-panic indicators. It automatically rejects new buy signals on days of market-wide crashes (e.g. EGX30 down >2%) or for illiquid stocks, protecting retail capital from sudden losses.
