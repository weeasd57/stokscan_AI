# Alternative Marketplaces for Pre-revenue Side Projects

Since Acquire.com and Microns.io require active traction and revenue, here are the top marketplaces that specialize in selling pre-revenue side projects, codebases, and starter SaaS platforms:

## 1. SideProjectors (sideprojectors.com) — Highly Recommended
* **Focus:** Specifically built for selling side projects, codebases, and SaaS tools that developers built but don't have time to market.
* **Why it fits:**
  * Free and easy to list.
  * No revenue requirements (pre-revenue projects are the norm).
  * Highly active developer and indie maker community looking for ready-made codebases.

## 2. Flippa (flippa.com)
* **Focus:** The largest global marketplace for buying and selling digital assets.
* **Why it fits:**
  * Has a dedicated category for **"Starter Sites"** and **"Templates & Code"**.
  * Reaches a massive audience of buyers looking for niche trading tools.
  * Instant listing approval.

## 3. Tiny Acquisitions (tinyacquisitions.com)
* **Focus:** A marketplace built specifically for buying and selling projects under $5,000.
* **Why it fits:**
  * Focuses entirely on early-stage, pre-revenue SaaS, APIs, and bots.
  * Lower entry barrier and transactional simplicity.
