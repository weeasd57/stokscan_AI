# Microns Step 4 Financial Details

Here are the recommended inputs for Step 4 of 7 on Microns.io:

---

### 1. Revenue type*
Select **"No revenue"** or **"Pre-revenue"** (or **"Subscription"** if you want to classify the intended business model).

### 2. What is your TTM Revenue, $?*
Enter **`0`** (If the platform enforces a non-zero number, select "No revenue" as type first, or enter `0` as the system allows starter kits/codebases).

### 3. Expenses, $*
Enter **`10`** (or write: `10 for domain name (egxbots.com)` if it's a text box, or just `10` if it's numeric).

### 4. Asking price, $*
Enter **`10000`** (representing your asking price of $10k).
